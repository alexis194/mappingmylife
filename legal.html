Mapping My Life

Privacy Policy

Private by Default: Your GPS coordinates, camera photos, and travel stops stay stored locally on your device. Nothing is visible to other travellers unless you explicitly choose + Add to My Public Travels.

Location & Camera Access: Location is used solely to display your pin on OpenStreetMap tiles. Camera access is used solely to compose your selfie postcard on-device.

Support

Need help with Mapping My Life, location permissions, or your travel stops? Contact us at support@mappingmylife.app.
